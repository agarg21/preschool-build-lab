## Decision / Exact Repair Brief

SOURCE_BACKED: supported wax paper, adult-demonstrated drop placement,
tilting as an alternative way to move drops, descriptive observation, general
water attention/emptying. MEASURED: repo/live/browser baseline only.
RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS: task lenses below. EDITORIAL_JUDGMENT: one plain-water
drop in a shallow nonbreakable rimmed tray lined with a flat wax-paper piece,
secured at dry edges by adult-applied owned tape. Complete set also includes
an adult-only intact dropper, a small water cup and towel. Adult prepares and
checks attached paper/tray grip/visible drop before starting, stores cup/tool/
spares away. No straw, mouth blowing, toothpick, dye, maze, timed race, size
contest or large pool in this default. Keep old URL; visible Water Drop Tilt
would honestly describe the reduced job rather than imply two-person racing.

Adult stays beside all reachable children and helps hold both tray sides.
Child may tilt one edge slightly while the other rests on the stable table,
then lower it; watch whether the drop moves, stays or is unclear. Repeat one
small tilt or finish, never require a finish line or successful movement.
Watching/pointing while adult tilts is an option; decline if tray grip/owned
materials/paper adhesion/drop visibility/every-child control/attention do not
fit. No arbitrary angle, time, drop size, breath/clinical threshold invented.
Stop and collect for mouthing/reaching for the tool/cup/tape, loose paper,
jerking/unsafe tray control, spill, discomfort or shifted attention. If no
clear movement, flatten and end, not add more water or restore mouth tools.
Adult immediately empties the tray/cup out of reach, removes/stores loose
materials, dries the tray/work area and stores tools. These controls and the
one-tray reduction are editorial, not a tested safety method.
