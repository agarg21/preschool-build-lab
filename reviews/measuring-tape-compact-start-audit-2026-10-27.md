## Implementation-Ready Brief

RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS task: caregiver with age4-6 child chooses/declines a
tabletop comparison, knows exactly how the pretend toy moves, identifies one
common starting edge and two endpoints, accepts point/watch/unclear/end, and
cleans up without another broad search. Secondary stress: younger reachable
child, no counting/color naming, no clear distance judgement, tape/surface
mismatch or shifted attention. Critical missing control or unsupported promise
fails regardless of score. Not real parent/child testing.

EDITORIAL_JUDGMENT default:
- One intact lightweight owned nonmagnetic block, no small/detachableparts,
  used as a pretend toy; one large flat cardboard board adult is willing to
  tape; owned painter's tape handled ONLY by adult. No need to acquire a
  hopper, tape measure, ruler, unit blocks, ramp, spring toy or product.
- Adult checks owned block labels/condition/suitability for every reachable
  child. Follow tape guidance and check a hidden spot; use board on stable
  level table away from edges. If compatibility, board flatness, space,
  mouthing, clear marks or attention do not fit, skip rather than claim safe.
- Adult makes three short PARALLEL strips in a line along board: one start,
  one nearer destination, one farther destination with visibly different gaps.
  Leave room to put block beside each ending strip. Adult points to the
  start-strip edge FACING destinations and each destination edge FACING start.
  Those facing edges define the gaps; block position is not the measuring edge.
- Child lifts and gently places block BESIDE the near destination, then the
  far one, or points for adult/watch/declines. No child jumping, toy jumping,
  flicking, throwing, rolling or launch mechanism.
- Compare gaps from that SAME start edge to the two named destination edges.
  Point or say nearer/farther/unclear. Counting, numbers and spoken color names
  are unnecessary. One comparison is enough; no race, real jump distance,
  measured force or numeric unit result.
- If judgement is unclear, adult points along each gap once, or simply
  watch/end. Do not add a launcher, force repeats or claim learning from a
  correct response. Stop for peeling/chewing/wrapping tape, throwing/mouthing
  block, discomfort, surface change or diverted attention.
- Adult removes/collects every strip promptly, puts away block/tape/board and
  clears table. No surface protection promise. Research-backed/not-family-tested,
  open-ended/unmeasured time-mess and unknown understanding/learning/safety.
  Local source/limits disclosure explains visual-comparison source mechanism
  versus handplaced block/board/counts/roles/editorial controls.

SOURCE_BACKED is common-reference comparison and labels/material context.
The exact static three-strip recipe is editorial reduction, not an inspected
source procedure. Numerical measurement would require an independently
qualified consistent-unit workflow; it is deliberately NOT this default.
