## Decision And Evidence

Replace the unsupported rescue/heat start with one plain-ice observation at
the same ice-melt-rescue URL. Adult handles a plain cube, shallow nonbreakable
tray, spoon and towel; children watch or point from beyond reach. No added
water, heat, salt, colour, embedded toys or child tools. Notice shape/size and
liquid, or say not yet clear and finish. No full-melt wait or forced result.
Keep readiness, reach, attendance, skip and stop visible before child action;
adult empties and dries immediately. Timing and mess are not measured.
