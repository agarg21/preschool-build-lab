# Sink or Float Tray: first-start and result audit

## Question and evidence limits

The named gap was whether a caregiver can select a suitable object, control a shallow-water setup, know when to postpone/stop, and recognize a floating rather than bottom-resting result *before* following the current four child-facing steps. The [NAEYC family science example](https://www.naeyc.org/our-work/families/support-science-learning%20) (undated page, accessed 2026-10-24) supports putting objects in water, asking for a prediction and discussing sink/float; its large-bowl/bucket and coin/key/rock examples do **not** certify KAL's shallow tray or an object set for a household with a younger child. The [AAP's May 2026 water guidance](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/news/Pages/AAP-releases-updated-drowning-prevention-recommendations.aspx) supports constant, attentive adult supervision near standing water and not leaving a young child alone; the [AAP infant container guidance](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/safety-prevention/at-play/Pages/Infant-Water-Safety.aspx) supports emptying open water containers after use. Neither tested this exact activity. [CPSC toy guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/id/Safety-Education/Toys) (undated page, accessed 2026-10-24) calls for age/ability fit and keeping small parts away from under-threes; it does not define every household object as safe. A [PBS sink/float result snippet](https://www.pbs.org/parents/crafts-and-experiments/sink-or-float-experiment/) describes top-versus-bottom observation, but the opened body required JavaScript, so that snippet is supplementary, not a fully inspected source.

Classes: those public statements are `SOURCE_BACKED` within the stated limits; live DOM, route/viewport measurements and byte equality are `MEASURED` technical evidence only; the caregiver task is `RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS`; proposed order, proxy scores and priority are `EDITORIAL_JUDGMENT`. Family comprehension, object suitability in practice, physical safety, actual duration/mess, engagement, learning, repeated use and SEO effect are `UNKNOWN`. The static card's "5 min" and "medium" mess have no retained measured basis in the owner, so they must not be treated as observed outcomes.

## Frozen persona task and acceptance

Source-grounded lens: one caregiver and an approximately four-year-old considering a prediction game at a table with a shallow tray. A younger child who can reach small objects or the water is the secondary stress; adult attention may be interrupted. These are planning constraints, not a real family. Required outputs: (1) decide start versus postpone given water/reach/attention; (2) choose a bounded, water-compatible object set without copying NAEYC's small examples into a younger child's reach; (3) adult sets/controls the tray; (4) child predicts, tests one object, and distinguishes staying up from resting at the bottom; (5) ambiguous result has a simple rescue/stop; (6) adult empties and puts away water/objects. Observable proxy success requires those outputs in the existing card/collection-to-card route without another broad search, with the adult stop before any executable child step. A critical missing before-action control or valid-result cue fails regardless of total score.
