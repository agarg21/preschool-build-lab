## Frozen Proposed Flat Default And Lineage

Only if appropriate for EVERY reachable child: two intact LARGE tiles from
one already-owned identifiable age-labelled set, one square and one triangle
that the adult can lay flat under control. Follow owned warnings/use/recall
notices and inspect all faces/edges/joins for cracks, damage, loose/missing
parts or exposed magnets. No tiny/mini parts, loose magnets/balls/rods/cubes,
mixed-set compatibility assumption, dismantling, taping repairs or purchase.
Unknown identity/labels/condition or uncontrolled younger reach means decline;
inspection, brand name and supervision are NOT certification.

Adult clears a firm low dry level space away from traffic/edges and stays
beside every reachable child; controls both tiles and spares. Skip for
mouthing/rough handling/unavailable attention. Try when child can lay a piece
gently and pause on request, otherwise adult places while child watches/points.
Adult first lays square flat and triangle flat outside ONE square edge,
triangle's broad edge facing square and opposite corner pointing away.
A small gap is fine; NO magnetic join, snap, force, upright wall or supported
roof is required. If pieces pull together/repel or cannot remain flat under
adult control, separate/leave a gap or end. No strength/stability result claimed.

Script: "This square is the house picture. Which way could the roof point?"
Kid may place the triangle flat, point, watch or decline; no naming/counting/
storytelling expectation. Notice body/roof as a picture, not a toy enclosure:
nothing goes inside and there is NO opening door. Move one flat piece apart
to reset if wanted, or end. Adult controls separation, counts/collects BOTH,
checks for damage and stores tiles/spares away. Stop for mouthing, throwing,
damage/missing parts/exposed magnets, discomfort, uncontrolled movement,
uncertain reach or shifted attention. Damaged/uncertain material means end
and follow owned manufacturer guidance, not repair or continue. No ingestion
diagnosis/remedy added; ordinary source referral must not become medical advice.
