## One Mechanism And Implementation-Ready Brief

RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS persona: caregiver of an age4-6 child wants to decide
whether to try a weight/balance observation, assemble it without another broad
search, know who loads/holds, distinguish an observation from a valid
measurement, accept unclear/end, and clean up.
Secondary stress: younger child can reach materials, attention changes,
empty cups hang unevenly, or the child cannot/will not speak, wait or touch.
Required outputs are complete materials, adult construction, fit/decline,
empty baseline, load/unload roles, observation limits, rescue/stop and storage.
A critical missing control fails regardless of rubric total. Proxy, not a
real parent/child test.

Frozen lineage: PBS SoCal matching paper-cup/two-hole handle construction;
PDF central support and symmetric geometry; PBS empty-level principle.
Do NOT silently combine PBS's different threaded-loop construction with the
selected handle recipe. The following exact low-load/adult/stop arrangement
is EDITORIAL_JUDGMENT reduction, not a source-tested procedure.

- Need one intact owned PLASTIC notched hanger, two matching intact paper cups,
  yarn, adult scissors and hole punch, and two intact lightweight nonmagnetic
  owned objects that fit inside without forcing. Each object must suit every
  reachable child; no small detachable pieces or loose fillers/coins/beans/
  marbles. Do not replace with wire, heavy baskets or unknown attachments.
- Adult does ALL cutting, punching, tying and inspection away from children.
  Two opposite holes at matching heights near each rim, two equal yarn pieces
  about one foot/30cm each, each end tied through opposite holes to form a
  handle; one handle on each symmetric notch, equal hanging length. Source
  dimensions describe construction, NOT a safety threshold/load rating.
  Check rims, knots, hanger condition and retention. If uncertain, skip.
- Adult alone holds the central hook without pinning the horizontal bar level,
  over a clear level floor. Keep assembly low while allowing cups to hang
  freely, not touch floor/each other. Exact grip and low-height rule are
  editorial clarifications of central support, not tested ergonomics.
  No doors, cabinet mounts, overhanging pencils, books or child-held suspension.
- Child only watches or points from beyond reach of yarn/hanger/moving cups,
  or declines. Adult must attend to every reachable child; skip if that
  separation/full attention cannot be maintained. Do not give a cord to a
  child, wrap it around a body, climb or pull on the assembly.
- Start EMPTY, let motion settle, inspect whether cups are roughly level and
  freely suspended. If uneven, touching, binding or unclear, lower until cups
  rest on floor and end. Do not add counterweights or manually force level.
  This is a decline/check, not calibration.
- Lower cups to rest on floor BEFORE adding/removing anything. Adult puts at
  most one checked object into each cup, then lifts centrally and lets movement
  settle. No one-handed loading while dangling and no child reaching into cups.
- Invite point/watch: left lower, right lower, roughly level, or unclear.
  Working balances can compare loads, but this uncalibrated homemade assembly
  does NOT establish equal mass from near-level cups, numeric weights or a
  guaranteed heavier result. Grip/friction/geometry and material variation
  remain limitations. One observation is enough; no compulsory rebalancing.
- End for reaching/pulling/mouthing/throwing, torn rims/loose knots/bending,
  swinging/contact, discomfort, divided attention or an unclear baseline.
  Adult lowers cups onto floor before handling, removes objects, disassembles
  yarn handles and stores ALL hanger/yarn/tools/containers/objects out of reach;
  clear floor. Never leave it suspended unattended.
- Local disclosure: research-backed, not family-tested, open-ended time and
  unmeasured mess/effort. Source mechanism differs from editorial roles,
  one-per-cup and floor-supported loading/stop controls. No safety guarantee,
  child outcome or exact material strength claim.

No substitution is mandatory: incompatible materials/control mean decline,
not improvise a mount. The usefulness hypothesis is an intelligible
