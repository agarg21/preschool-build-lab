## One Decision

Keep Color Mixing Cups title/URL; replace two filled cups/pour together with
two prepared source colours and a third initially empty receiving cup. Complete
materials: three clear nonbreakable cups, water, owned red/yellow food colouring,
adult spoon, rimmed tray and towel. Adult prepares per owned labels away from
children, stores colouring/caps away, checks distinguishable sources and room
for two small spoonfuls or declines. Adult transfers yellow then red into the
receiver and gently mixes once. Child watches/points/describes different,
same or unclear; naming a colour is optional. Fit/reach/attendance/skip/stop
precede action; end without adding dye to force a result, immediately empty,
rinse, dry and store. Timing/mess/staining unmeasured; not family-tested.

URL, walking-water variant, product recommendation or paid call. The one
complete default repairs an existing parent task, not a new demand hypothesis.
