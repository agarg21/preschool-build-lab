## Decision / Evidence

Keep old water-drop-race URL; visible Water Drop Tilt now describes one
supported plain-water drop task. Complete materials: shallow nonbreakable
rimmed tray, flat-fitting wax-paper piece, owned tape, adult-only intact
dropper, small water cup, plain unheated water and towel. Adult secures dry
paper edges, checks attachment/visible bead/grip/control or declines, and puts
cup/tool/spares away before child tray movement. Adult steadies far rim;
child holds near rim with both hands, lifts near edge a little while far edge
rests on table, then lowers. Watch route: adult holds both rims. Say/point
moved/stayed/unclear. Repeat once only if moved; otherwise flatten/end.
Early fit/skip/stop; immediate empty/collect/dry/store. No mouth tool, dye,
toothpick, finish line, timed competition or bigger-drop requirement.
