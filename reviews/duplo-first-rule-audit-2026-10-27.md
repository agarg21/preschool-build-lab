Proposed editorial default: four intact large DUPLO bricks from an owned set,
two matching pairs that are distinguishable from each other. Adult builds one
two-brick tower; child uses the matching pair to copy the same bottom/top
arrangement beside it, model visible. Compare, gently separate and optionally
swap builder/copier. No timer, winner, recall demand, race, height target or
instruction to invent a rule. Pair requirement must be clear before starting;
if unsuitable parts/no pairs, skip rather than recommend a purchase.

Before action: adult checks owned labels/condition/fit for every reachable
child, clears low firm space, checks child can place/separate with help and
follow stop cue, stays beside all reachable children, keeps spares/small LEGO
out of younger reach and skips if unable to attend. Stop for mouthing,
throwing, damaged pieces, forceful separation/discomfort or unwanted collapse.
Adult handles stuck bricks; never teeth, pulling hard or forced continuation.
These are conservative editorial controls, not proven safe-in-practice claims.
Rescue: leave model visible, point to the next match or let adult place it;
use a side-by-side row if joining is unwanted. Collect/count all four, remove
damaged parts, store away after play. Duration/mess/outcomes unmeasured.
