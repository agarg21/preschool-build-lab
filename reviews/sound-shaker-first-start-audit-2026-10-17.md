## Parent Job And Evidence

`RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS` task: a caregiver of an approximately four-year-old has opaque household containers, rice and chunky blocks and wants a listening/matching game at a table. A younger child can reach the table while the adult's attention is divided. Before handing over any container, the caregiver needs to decide whether the materials and child fit, who fills and checks containers, how to create a matchable pair, the first shake/compare move, what to do when sounds do not match, and when to stop and clear materials. Success is a coherent start-or-postpone decision from the current route without another broad search; failure is a missing or late critical adult control, no matchable setup, or a route that implies tested safety.
