## Frozen Proposed Default And Lineage

Adult first checks every reachable child's abilities and owned material labels,
warnings and condition. One already-owned intact LARGE play-pom from an
age-labelled play set, a short clean/dry/intact lightweight open-ended paper
tube, one stable open shallow unbreakable receiver on a low clear surface.
No purchase, craft-pom certification, dimension threshold, squeezing,
cotton/food/marble/ball/small-part substitution, tape, cutting, tongs, paint or
wall installation. If labels/fit/condition/reach are uncertain, unsuitable,
or any reachable child mouths/tears the materials, decline this default.
Large alone is not sufficient; supervision does not override unsuitable parts.

Adult stays beside EVERY reachable child, holds tube with lower opening
clear above receiver and upper opening within comfortable reach, retaining
control of tube and all unused material. Adult first tries the ONE pom:
it must fall through freely without pushing/squeezing and land in receiver.
No suitable combination means stop, not force or change to smaller filler.
This trial tests only immediate passage, NOT safety or family usability.

Adult models once; kid mission: release one pom at top, watch it emerge below
and land. No child catching/speed/naming requirement. Adult retrieves and
offers the same single pom only after it has landed; optional repeat/end.
If stuck/not seen: stop child action; adult takes tube/pom out of child reach,
checks/collects the one item; no poking, shaking at people or squeezing.
One adult recheck only if same fit is plainly restored; otherwise end.
Child may watch/point while adult performs the drop, or decline texture/interest.
Stop for mouthing, tearing/shedding, throwing, uncertain reach, unstable receiver,
damage, lost attention, blocked passage or child request. Adult accounts for
the ONE pom, tube and receiver, removes damaged pieces and stores away.

## Persona And Four Traced Lenses

Caregiver of a preschool child has owned tubes and a play-pom, no usable
creator video, limited attention and a potentially reachable younger child.
Task: decide fit or decline, position/control ONE tube and receiver, trial,
release/watch/land, retrieve/reset or end, then account for all material.
Secondary stress: texture refusal, uncertain exit, interrupted attention and
320px enlarged text. Success requires all critical controls before action,
an observable first loop and one non-escalating rescue without another broad
search. These are research hypotheses, not observed family needs/outcomes.
