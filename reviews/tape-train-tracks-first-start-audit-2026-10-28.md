## Separately Registered Repair Brief

All exact reduction/quantity/geometry/roles/stop/fit rules editorial, not
source-tested protection. Do not silently implement within this research.

1. Adult chooses ONE intact large owned hand-pushed toy train suitable under
   its labels for EVERY reachable child. No magnets, batteries, cords,
   detachable/loose small parts, damage, motor/launch mechanism or linked
   train cars. No suitable owned train? Choose another activity; an existing
   large owned vehicle may use the separate Tape Road routine if its checks
   fit, not a train substitution or purchase recommendation.
2. Use ONE intact flat sacrificial cardboard board, fully supported on a low
   stable clear surface away from edges/walkways. Adult chooses an owned
   removable painter tape whose instructions fit the proposed use; read and
   follow its labels/removal limits. No household floor/furniture default,
   electrical/duct/stronger tape, blades, tools, heat or chemicals.
   Adult stays beside every reachable child and controls roll/strips/spares.
   Skip mouthing, uncontrolled younger reach, unavailable attention or inability
   to pause. Point/watch/decline; no forced touch or train sounds.
3. BEFORE child access, adult checks one short tape piece on that board,
   presses/removes gently per label, and clears bits. Paper may tear; this is
   not a finish/no-damage or later-removal guarantee. If tape lifts, sheds or
   fit/label is uncertain, choose an untaped board route or end.
   Lay TWO short straight parallel tape lines, spaced wider than the actual
   train, and ONE short cross-strip joining them at the far end: station mark.
   All strips flat, no loose edges, all within board and comfortable reach.
   No ties, loop, crossing, extra props or physical track constraint.
4. Adult gently pushes train along the clear gap to the station and stops;
   wheels run on cardboard BETWEEN the lines, not on tape rails. Trial must
   move freely without force/catching or board shifting; failedfit means
   untaped route/watch/end, not more tape or motor force. Return to open end.
   Say: Take the train between these lines. Stop at this station mark.
5. Child pushes once toward station, or points for adult/watch/decline.
   Stopping elsewhere is a valid finish, not error scoring. No required route
   change, sounds, precision, speed, repeated trip or longer engagement.
   Adult may reset only if wanted; no adding pieces/higher build.
6. Stop for tape peeling/chewing/wrapping, toy mouthing/throwing/damage/loose
   parts, forced motion, board moving, materials leaving setup, discomfort,
   surface change or attention shift. Adult collects train/ALL THREE strips/
   roll/bits, checks surface/floor, follows owned tape removal guidance, and
   stores all out of reach; damaged board discarded/cleared by adult.
   No unattended accessible setup. Unknown setup/duration/mess/outcomes.
