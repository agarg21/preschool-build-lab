## Bounded Implementation Brief

Register separately. Use existing `page_activity`/`activity_overrides` for
the independent hub's Cup entry only, not global ACTIVITIES changes that
would rewrite other hubs. One adult-guided default: two matching intact
lightweight paper cups, optional spare; low firm clear surface, adult checks
balance and demonstrates bottom-to-bottom, child removes/replaces gently.
Adult stays beside all children who can reach the setup; skip if unable to
attend or keep spare/pieces out of younger reach. Stop after mouthing,
throwing, damaged cups, climbing or unwanted collapse. Put away whole set/
remove damaged pieces, no safety assurance. Reuse maintained guide's details,
not a newly invented material certification or promised result.

Hub: clear adult role before action, matching row/summary, early guide route,
open-ended/unmeasured labels, one rescue/put-away or direct depth link.
Card: early fit/skip/stop/guide route before steps, retaining same default and
credit. Guide: move existing stop before quick-start steps and ensure direct
first-try adult-role/stop order. Keep remaining body/image/other activities.
