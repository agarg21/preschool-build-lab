## Frozen Default And Lineage

One already-owned, lightweight empty paper-towel roll, clean/dry/intact and
without staples, sharp edges, loose covering or attached parts, subject to an
adult's fit judgment for all reachable children. No hard mailing tube,
packaging with a blade, loose toy, ball, filling, weight, cord, tape, scissors,
glue or ramp required. If no suitable roll or child mouths/tears it, skip;
do not recommend a purchase or promise a sanitizing method.

Adult clears a low level floor space away from steps/traffic, stays beside
all reachable children and skips when unable to attend. Put the empty roll
on its curved side, opening sideways; adult models a gentle push along the
floor. Kid mission: push the empty roll and watch it move. Retrieve after it
stops, return it to its side and repeat only if wanted; no distance, speed,
target, competition or expected engagement. If it does not move, adult checks
that it is on its side and the space is clear, then gently models; adult may
push while child watches/points. If unsuitable or unwanted, stop rather than
add small toys, a slope, force or another complicated fix. Stop for mouthing,
tearing, damage, throwing, unsafe reach or lost adult attention. Adult collects
roll/spares, removes damaged material and stores away; time/mess unmeasured.
