## Separately Registered Repair Brief

All exact quantities, roles, exclusion/skip/stop and fit rules below are
EDITORIAL_JUDGMENT, not validated protection or a source-tested recipe.
Titles/URLs retained; key parent job is sort, then optionally stack low.

1. Adult selects FOUR intact large basic DUPLO bricks from an owned set:
   same compatible shape/footprint, two same-color pairs, the two colors
   visibly distinguishable to the participating child. Follow owned set
   age/warning guidance for EVERY child who could reach them, not only the
   named player. No standard small LEGO, loose/detachable small parts,
   magnets, battery pieces, damaged pieces, tools or mixed-family substitute.
   No suitable owned set? Choose another activity, not a purchase.
2. Low stable clear surface away from edges/walkways; adult beside every
   reachable child, spares/small LEGO out of reach. Skip mouthing, uncontrolled
   younger reach, unavailable attention or inability to pause. Color naming
   and counting not required; point/watch/decline enough, no forced touch.
   Before child access adult gently checks each pair joins/stays and can be
   separated without teeth, force or tools, then lays all four loose again.
   Failed fit means flat pairs/end,
   not pressure or smaller pieces. Research-backed/not-family-tested visible.
3. Put ONE seed of each color side by side, separated, both visible. Retain
   the other two as choices. If an adult model helps, place a matching choice
   beside its seed, then return it to the choices; leave both seeds in place.
   child chooses one remaining brick to put beside its matching-color seed,
   or points for adult placement. Offer the last brick or finish. No quiz,
   memory cover, speed rule, compulsory spoken color/number or scored error.
4. Optional low-build mission: join just the two matching bricks into one
   two-brick stack, then the other pair if wanted. Adult steadies/supports
   and handles separation; gentle placement only. Keep every brick on the
   surface, no height escalation or required repeat. If joining/choosing is
   difficult, leave matching flat pairs, watch the adult or end. If color
   distinctions do not fit, use the flat/watch/decline alternative rather
   than adding an unqualified shape-test or correcting the child.
5. Stop for mouthing, throwing, damage/loose parts, forceful pulling, collapse/
   discomfort, pieces leaving the surface or attention shift. Adult collects
   all FOUR and any bits, checks surface/floor, gently separates without teeth/
   tools, stores all out of reach and clears the area. Unknown time/effort/mess;
   no unattended accessible setup. No blanket DUPLO-nearby assurance.
