## Bounded Separate Repair Brief

Canonical base: one clean empty dry intact already-open small cardboard box
and one intact large lightweight owned toy car appropriate under labels for
every child who can reach it. Adult inspects for staples, sharp/damaged edges,
packing/plastic/loose parts and chooses another box or skips rather than fixes
hazards during play. These box/attention/cleanup checks are conservative KAL
EDITORIAL_JUDGMENT, not an inspected/approved physical setup.

Adult clears low floor space away from walkways, sets box on its side with
opening facing player and unobstructed, and checks one car can enter and
leave without force, cutting or lifting the box. Toys only: no sitting,
climbing, wearing or shutting anyone inside. Adult stays beside every
reachable child, keeps spare parts/tools away; skip if fit/space/attention
cannot be maintained. No scissors, tape, door-making or decoration needed
for the base. The adult's readiness choice replaces age-only assurance.

One small mission: park the car in the pretend garage and drive it back out.
Three short steps: drive car through ready opening; park/say or point where
it is; drive out through same opening, repeat or finish. If it catches, park
beside the box or watch/point while adult models; do not cut a new entrance
or force a car through. Stop for mouthing, throwing, torn/sharp cardboard,
loose parts, feet/climbing/body entry, blocked paths or lost adult attention.
Adult puts box/car away and clears walking area. Open-ended/time and mess
unmeasured, research-backed/not-family-tested, physical outcomes UNKNOWN.

Preserve engineering's optional two-car task: two appropriate cars must already
fit side by side with a clear exit; adult prepares/handles any short bay tape,
keeps spares away and checks tape/box fit. Place all fit/attend/skip/stop BEFORE
mission/steps. Child parks two cars, identifies adult-prepared bays and rolls
one out without moving the other. First change is adult-adjusted bay spacing,
a different already-open box or one-car rescue, never widening/cutting during
play. Preserve three steps/test, existing ID/chooser/arrival and peer builds.
Source-note clearly distinguishes PBS parking-line principle from this KAL
two-car editorial extension and Purdue from the base. No tape-performance
assurance or motor/learning benefit. Base card link names simpler one-car start.
