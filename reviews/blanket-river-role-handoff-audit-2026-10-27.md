## Bounded Default And Lineage

Owned materials: a blanket that stays flat outside walkways; three intact
large lightweight blocks (two low wide supports/one longer span); one intact
large lightweight toy suitable for every reachable child under maker guidance.
No cushions, chairs, stools, boards, body crossing, hidden fillers, small
figures, loose magnets or battery parts needed. Skip if material/space/attention
does not fit, rather than recommend buying or invent a substitute recipe.

Adult clears low floor space away from doors/traffic and lays blanket flat.
Build a tiny crossing near one edge, not across the blanket's full width:
two low blocks a short gap apart, longer block resting on both. Adult checks
both ends rest on supports without force. Stay beside every reachable child,
keep spares away and say toys only, never feet. Child may place with help,
watch or point. Skip if stop cue or adult attention cannot be maintained.

Mission: help the toy cross the pretend river. Carry it in a hand over the
bridge and back, rather than asking the build to bear its weight. Reset toy,
repeat or finish voluntarily. If span does not fit, lay blocks flat as a toy
path; do not widen the gap, stack higher, add heavy objects or force. Stop for
mouthing, throwing, damaged parts, blanket bunching/slipping, running/climbing,
feet on route, blocked paths, lost attention or unwanted collapse. Adult clears
blanket/blocks/toy from the walking area after play. Time/mess unmeasured.
