## Decision

Keep October 4's **conditional discoverable-depth** gate, but add a **before-action agreement** gate for an entry surface that itself gives executable steps. Freeze the parent's required material, adult role, stop/decline and first move for that surface. Before its first executable child step, the surface must offer a source-bounded start-or-decline decision and must not contradict its maintained depth owner. If a critical output remains guide-held, show a plainly labeled, usable route to that exact owner before the steps; verify readable arrival under the relevant viewport/text/input stress. A self-contained card with the critical outputs present does not need an early deep link. An optional extension is a separate task, not a defect in a deliberately narrow start.

This is an `EDITORIAL_JUDGMENT` review rule, not a claim that parents scan in a certain order or that these repairs improved family use. A guide link alone cannot launder conflicting or late instructions on an executable shortcut. The rule fails if a future source-grounded entry-to-start task passes the recorded material/role/stop agreement and route checks but still lacks a critical decision without another broad search. It is too broad if an early link or repeated warning obscures an otherwise complete, source-bounded local start. Inspect that counterexample before expanding the rule to other cards.

Observable proxy success is a coherent choice and runnable first round/reset without another broad search; failure is missing or conflicting setup/rule/stop, an inaccessible route, or a necessary output hidden after action.
