## Week In Review

### Repeated learning

- A compact card can be useful for execution while still failing a complete
  parent decision. The Paper Bridge card scored 7 of 24; the dedicated guide
  scored 23 of 24 without inventing duration.
- First-screen fit, materials, adult role, first action, and stop information
  repeatedly matter more than generic introductory prose.
- Dominant visuals function as instructions. Both recent guide transactions
  needed exact visual-text agreement and explicit illustration provenance.
- Later sections earn their place through comparison, troubleshooting,
  adaptation, cleanup, or evidence limits. Length itself is not quality.

### Contradiction resolved

The user wants more high-quality individual activities, while the evidence
rules prohibit manufacturing pages from cadence or one page's traffic. The
resolution is a continuous research pipeline, not a publishing quota: research
the next candidate while Paper Bridge is observed, then publish at most one
only if it independently clears every gate.

## Falsifiable Publication Rule

Research for the next dedicated guide may start during Paper Bridge's
observation window. Implementation is eligible only when one candidate from
the maintained activity inventory has all of the following:

1. a current exact-query family and live SERP showing one singular activity
   job rather than a plural roundup or wording variant;
2. no clean fit in an existing canonical owner;
3. a current source layer supporting setup, materials, supervision, and stop
   boundaries without firsthand or outcome claims;
4. maintainable KAL information gain such as a first start, substitution,
   controlled comparison, rescue, adaptation, or explanatory visual;
5. a reproducible current-task baseline and a proposed parent-first page that
   can score at least 22 of 24 on desktop and mobile without avoidable clutter;
6. no edit to a protected page and no dependence on product, medical,
   developmental, universal-safety, or family-tested claims.

If no candidate clears every condition, the research result is
`REJECT_HYPOTHESIS` and no page is created. A candidate that passes may produce
one separately registered implementation, never a batch.
