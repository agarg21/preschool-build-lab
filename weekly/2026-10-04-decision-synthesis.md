## Test September 27 Rule

The [previous synthesis](2026-09-27-owner-and-start-synthesis.md) required measured, visually checked arrival for changed handoffs beneath sticky headers. This week, the Ball Maze true-hash route failed and was repaired with a text-relative offset, while the ramp enlarged heading/route also needed correction. The prior rule remains useful: a working href and no overflow were insufficient. Its prospective effectiveness across future releases is not yet measured; these repairs are not controlled comparisons. The Ball Maze card-only audit exposed a separate question the rule did not settle: how much scanning happens **before** the parent reaches the qualifying link.

The new conditional rule is falsifiable. It fails if a reviewed card with a missing critical output has an early, plainly labeled guide route and passes recorded entry/arrival geometry, yet a concrete source-grounded task still cannot find or use the required output without another broad search; inspect route label, ordering and target, then revise the gate. It is also too broad if a future card with a complete local start and stop is made worse by moving a nonessential guide link ahead of execution. The next Tape Road audit is a test of applicability, not presumed confirmation.

Record card-only critical outputs, link label and position relative to media/steps/stop, actual keyboard/touch route, target heading visibility, viewport and text mode. At desktop and mobile normal text plus mobile 200% root text, state `PRESERVE` when the parent can decide/start/stop and reach depth without an avoidable detour; state `IMPROVE` only for a reproduced material gap. Keep the card and guide scores separate, with their own denominators and N/A reasons. A late pixel coordinate alone is not a human-abandonment measure.
