Condition5 of the [September13 synthesis](2026-09-13-guide-first-synthesis.md)
generalized a fixed 22/24 threshold. Its denominator is not universal:
helicopter evaluates all13 dimensions for26 possible points. A total also
cannot prove a critical cut, stop or substitution is correctly explained.

Prospectively replace that condition with the full13-dimension rubric in
[the persona protocol](../reviews/persona-review-protocol.md). Record each
applicable score, source/observed basis, N/A reason and denominator; do not
compare totals across different tasks/rubrics. Define required planning
outputs and critical instructions first. Every critical instruction must be
present, consistent, appropriately evidenced and executable in the evaluated
desktop/mobile task. Any critical failure blocks release regardless of score.
Independent P0-P2 correction and all other publication gates remain mandatory.

A missing existing page means no before-page baseline, not an invented zero.
For an existing owner, keep the reproducible before-task evidence. A low or
changed total is a prompt to inspect dimensions, never a reason to relax
critical gates or claim satisfaction. Historical task-specific criteria stay
historical; this does not retroactively rescore or invalidate released pages.
