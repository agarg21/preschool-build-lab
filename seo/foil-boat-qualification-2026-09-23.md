## Frozen Existing-Owner Brief And Every-Section Gates

One no-cargo default: adult prepares a square of foil, towel and shallow tray
on a stable wipeable surface. Fold edges upward, pinch corners without cutting,
retain an open cavity with broad flat bottom. Adult demonstrates/handles the
boat as needed. Check empty flotation/clearance, notice lean or water entry,
optionally change one side and retry, or finish. Child participation may be
watching/choosing; wet handling is optional. Timing and engagement UNKNOWN.

This is an EDITORIAL_JUDGMENT simplification of the source empty-hull stage,
not a source-tested preschool activity or a complete capacity experiment.
Adult remains beside and attends to the setup continuously. Stop for mouthing,
torn loose foil, spills/unstable setup, loss of attention or desire to leave;
adult gathers foil, drains and stores the tray and wipes spills. If supervision
cannot continue, stop and remove access to water. No rule makes it universally
safe. Adult preparation/child observer roles do not substitute for supervision.
