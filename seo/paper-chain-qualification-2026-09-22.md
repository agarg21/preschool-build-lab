## Frozen Improvement Brief

One coherent upgrade, not a broad card-template redesign. Proposed default is
an untimed linked chain made from ordinary paper and tape, adult-prepared
strips, optional child joining/choosing/counting/watching. No staples, string,
wearing, hanging across routes or body-wrap target. These are conservative
editorial limits, not official safety certification.

The future page must state: form and close the first loop; pass an OPEN strip
through it; bring that strip's own ends together and tape them; repeat through
the newest loop. An original three-frame diagram must show threading before
closure and distinct interlocked rings, not a flattened strip or glued overlap
between two rings. Its prose alternative must be sufficient without images.
An adult-created sample is an editorial explanation, never evidence of testing.

Preparation gate: choose and label one practical strip geometry as an editorial
starting suggestion, not a source-tested optimum. Adult should be able to curl
it and overlap its own ends without forcing. S2's ambiguous dimension wording
must not be laundered into precision. Validate text/diagram geometry at build
review; physical fit and child dexterity remain unknown.

Recovery is editorial mechanism reasoning: if circles are separate, leave the
next strip open until threaded; if a closure lifts, adult reattaches that same
joint; replace torn paper for free building or finish. For a strict one-sheet
trial, no new paper is added: repair with original pieces or record that the
trial ended. A fresh-sheet retry is a new trial. If repeated joining needs the
adult to do all work, offer observing/choosing or stop, not a success promise.
Keep scissors with the adult; stop mouthing, pulling around bodies or unsafe
movement; gather tape/strips/offcuts at the end. No universal age suitability.

Optional length trial uses one sheet per attempt, the same sheet size/type
for any comparison, tape to close loops rather than extend their length, and
interlocked rings only. Lay down gently from a common starting line without
stretching; counting links does not equal measuring length when sizes differ.
No winner, stopwatch, ruler, printable or optimum-width claim is required.
Changing rules is allowed but starts a different challenge; do not compare it
as the same trial. This is KAL's explicit rule choice, not a universal definition.
