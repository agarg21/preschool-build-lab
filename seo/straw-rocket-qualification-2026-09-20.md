## Parent Workflow Brief

1. Decide fit: printer available, adult can prepare and launch, clear direction
   maintained. Present these constraints before optional science or variants.
2. Adult prints the linked source template, cuts and assembles the R1 model.
   Provide a concise original assembly explanation with attribution. Check
   complete source correspondence rather than reproducing the worksheet.
3. Confirm sliding fit and taped closure. Adult retains launcher; child may
   decorate before assembly, count down or watch. No promise of engagement.
4. Start one adult-controlled launch, then pause. The child mission is to
   notice where it lands; measurement is optional after a workable start.
5. For a stuck sleeve or leak, stop and inspect away from the mouth, then
   remake or retape. Clear paper scraps/tape and put tools away at finish.

Supervision, retrieval sequencing and damaged-material stops are conservative
editorial boundaries, not evidence that the activity is safe in practice.
No medically framed breath technique, inhalation experiment or distance target.
No no-printer, metal-straw or wider-sleeve fallback in this initial version;
each would change fit or source assumptions. A failed fit is a stop/remake
decision, not permission for stronger blowing. Timings are UNKNOWN; describe
preparation work without invented minutes. No more than one optional repeat.
