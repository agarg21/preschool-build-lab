## Maintained Inventory Audit

### Selected Lead 1: Science / Observe

**Parent-facing choice:** Make a shadow change.

**Future same-page anchor:** `#shadow-change`

**Maintained inventory source:** `shadow-shape-match` plus the current
`Shadow Builder` section. The future broad page must contain the complete
runnable lead; neither legacy destination is the required task-completion
surface.

**Materials:** A flashlight, one large opaque block or household object, and a
blank wall.

**Adult role:** Put the flashlight on a stable surface pointing at the wall,
dim only enough to see the shadow, and keep the walking path clear. Do not aim
the light at a face.

**Kid mission:** Move the object closer to and farther from the light to make
one larger shadow and one smaller shadow.

**Readiness cue:** Use this start when the child can follow a one-step stop cue
and move one large object while the light stays still. The adult operates the
flashlight if the child points it toward people or cannot leave it stable.

**Stop and reset:** Stop after the two contrasting shadows or sooner if the
light will not stay pointed at the wall; switch off the light and restore the
walking path.

**Source boundary:** Different objects/light sources and changing shadows are
source-backed by Smithsonian. Stable placement, the two-shadow default,
clear-path check, exact wording, and ordering are KAL editorial guidance.
Engagement, comprehension, timing, learning, and safe completion are unknown.

### Selected Lead 2: Engineering / Build And Test

**Parent-facing choice:** Build and test a paper bridge.

**Future same-page anchor:** `#paper-bridge-test`

**Maintained inventory source:** `paper-bridge` plus the current `Bridge
Rescue` section. The future broad page must contain the complete runnable lead;
neither legacy destination is the required task-completion surface.

**Materials:** One sheet of paper, two broad closed books, and one large light
object such as a block, plastic lid, or toy.

**Adult role:** Put one book flat on each side of a short floor-level gap and
keep the supports low and stable. The child places the paper and tests one
object.

**Kid mission:** Span the gap, test the flat paper, then fold the paper once
and compare one stronger version if the first bends.

**Readiness cue:** Use this start when the child can place one sheet across a
gap and release one light object without lifting, climbing on, or striking the
books. The adult owns the supports and ends the setup if that boundary does
not hold.

**Stop and reset:** Stop after one flat and one folded test. End the setup if
books become climbing, throwing, or finger-trapping objects; return the books
and paper when done.

**Source boundary:** Head Start specifically describes an adult-supported
preschool bridge investigation and comparison after loading; TERC supports
the create/test/improve loop. Two closed books, the short gap, one light load,
one fold, stop rule, and substitutions are KAL editorial guidance. Holding
capacity, duration, learning, enjoyment, and safe completion are unknown.

### Selected Lead 3: Math / Compare And Continue

**Parent-facing choice:** Continue a repeating pattern.

**Future same-page anchor:** `#continue-a-pattern`

**Maintained inventory source:** `pattern-path`. The future broad page must
contain the complete runnable lead; the legacy card is not the required
task-completion surface.

**Materials:** Two colors or types of large blocks, tiles, household objects,
or adult-prepared paper squares. Use pieces appropriate for the youngest child
who can reach them.

**Adult role:** Start an `A-B-A-B` sequence with four pieces, name the two
parts once, and ask what comes next without supplying the answer immediately.

**Kid mission:** Add the next piece, say or point to the repeating part, and
extend the path once.

**Readiness cue:** Use this start when the child can match two visibly
different pieces and place one next item. If repeating the sequence is not yet
manageable, the adult switches to matching two alike pieces and does not call
that result a completed pattern.

**Stop and reset:** Stop after one correct extension or one supported retry;
collect every loose piece at cleanup.

**Source boundary:** NAEYC supports concrete objects and paper squares for
creating, copying, correcting, and extending repeating patterns. The two-color
`A-B` default, one-extension stop, material substitutions, and adult script
are KAL editorial guidance. Independence, comprehension, duration,
engagement, and learning are unknown.
