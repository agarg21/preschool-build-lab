## Decision

No exact first-party query row or current numeric search estimate establishes material demand for a new KAL URL. Do not build or add lacing to the fine-motor page from this evidence alone.

This is a rejection of **present publication eligibility**, not evidence that the activity is ineffective or that no one searches for it. A new, independently supported exact job plus a substantive output absent from current sources would reopen the decision. No top-five likelihood is inferred.

## Persona Task And Source Boundaries

Proxy task from RES029: a caregiver with a preschooler around four and a small carpeted table wants one quiet, reusable, dry craft. They must choose whether cardboard/shoelace lacing fits, prepare it, show the first move, help when threading stalls, then stop and store it. Secondary stress: a younger child can reach the table and the adult has limited attention. Required output is a go/no-go decision on adult preparation and reach, a default first hole, one easier adjustment, a stall response, and cleanup, without promising independent or lengthy engagement. Success for a proposed KAL page would require a distinct source-reconciled answer to all these outputs that current results do not already give; failure is repeated instructions with unsupported safety/outcome claims. This is not a tested caregiver task or scored site-page review.

Candidate mechanism **for research comparison only**, not a published recommendation: adult cuts a broad cardboard shape and punches a few spaced holes; use one shoelace without beads/buttons or needle; model the first thread; offer fewer/wider-spaced holes if the start is hard; stop and put the card/lace away when the setup no longer fits. Cardboard, lace and first demonstration are supported by the opened activity bodies; fewer/spaced holes by Learning Jungle. The no-bead/no-needle choice, stop/reset and storage are KAL editorial controls, not observed safety performance. Adult tool handling is a conservative editorial boundary, not an externally established requirement for every child. [CPSC strings/straps guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/safety-education/safety-guides/toys/strings-and-straps-toys-can-strangle-young-children) and its [birth-through-five toy guide](https://www.cpsc.gov/s3fs-public/2_0.pdf) support caution about cords around young children and keeping older-child materials away from younger children; they do **not** certify a lace length, age, setup or supervision level as safe. With younger-child reach and limited adult attention, the task may be a no-go. No numeric cord rule is invented.

Current KAL [fine-motor collection](../site/collections/fine-motor-activities-for-preschoolers.html) contains broad drop/stack/scoop/build choices and no lacing; [indoor chooser](../site/collections/indoor-activities-for-preschoolers.html) routes broader movement/build/game needs; [card library](../site/cards.html) has no lacing card. This establishes a site-content absence, not an unmet search job. The fine-motor page's broad search owner should not be moved or expanded on this evidence. No new URL, card, source row or internal link is recommended.
