  silently attributed to another source, or the toy doorway is claimed to be
  physically validated without evidence.

## Claim Register And Reusable Rule

- Source-backed: stacking/rebuilding cups; paper cups in the measured design
  source; plastic cups in the concise play source; adult demonstration or
  supervision as actually described.
- Editorial/needs validation: fixed cup count, substituting material between
  sources, removing measuring/recording, quiet collapse/reset, and any
  cup-over-gap doorway. Not every editorial choice requires a family test, but
  it must have a defensible factual mechanism before publication.
- Unknown: exact timing, comprehension, enjoyment, engagement, repeatability,
  developmental or educational outcomes, mess, safety performance, and all
  firsthand KAL family experience. No material is declared universally safe.

Reusable lesson: reconcile the *goal and mechanism* before consolidating
activities that share materials. A calm guide needs one executable default;
measurement and toy-clearance can be separate, explicitly qualified extensions
only after they earn their evidence and scan cost.

Confidence: high for the inspected source differences and unchanged repository
ownership; medium for the proposed parent-job distinction; unknown for live
query intent, doorway feasibility, and family outcomes.
