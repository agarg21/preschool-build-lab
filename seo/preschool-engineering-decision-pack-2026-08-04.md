## Source-Backed Engineering Model

[TERC Head Start on Engineering](https://www.terc.edu/hse/everyday-engineering/)
describes engineering for young children as designing and testing ideas to
solve problems and presents an iterative Ask, Imagine, Plan, Create, Improve
cycle. [HeadStart.gov](https://www.headstart.gov/school-readiness/teacher-time-series/exploring-engineering-preschoolers)
similarly frames preschool engineering as finding a problem, imagining and
planning, creating, and improving, with adults supporting through questions
and open-ended materials.

These sources support a simple problem-test-redesign structure and a bounded
adult prompting role. They do not establish that KAL's activities were used,
safe in practice, engaging, educationally effective, or age-fit for every
child. KAL should adapt the model in original parent-facing language and cite
the sources rather than copy their wording or imply their results.
