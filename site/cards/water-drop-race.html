One drop | Supported tilt

Water Drop Tilt

Research by Riley Hart · AI research contributor

Need: 1 shallow nonbreakable rimmed tray, 1 wax-paper piece, owned tape, 1 adult dropper, 1 small water cup, plain unheated water, 1 towel.
Open-ended; duration and mess not measured.

Before you start

Materials: Adult checks intact tray and dropper and the owned tape's labels. Adult handles tape, dropper, cup and spares; keep them out of children's reach. No straw, blowing, dye, toothpick or tasting.

Adult and space: Use a stable level table away from edges, electronics and walking routes. Adult stays beside every reachable child and steadies the far rim throughout. Child holds the near rim with both hands; the far edge stays on the table.

Fit or skip: Try when the child can make a small supported move with help and follow a stop cue. Watching or pointing while the adult tilts is optional; adult holds both rims for this route. Skip if mouthing, grip, paper attachment, drop visibility or attention does not fit.

Evidence: Research-backed, not family-tested by Kid Activity Lab.

Stop: Lower the tray flat and end if paper loosens, the whole tray lifts off the table, grip slips, water escapes, a child reaches for tools or mouths materials, anyone is uncomfortable or attention shifts.

Adult setup: Fit a wax-paper piece flat inside the dry tray's rim. Adult tapes its dry edges down and checks it stays attached. Use the dropper to place one plain water drop near the middle. Skip if it soaks in or no bead is visible. Put the cup, dropper and spare materials out of reach, with the towel ready. Check adult and child can hold the named rims while one edge stays supported; otherwise watch only or skip.
1

Find the drop. Adult steadies the far rim; hold the near rim with both hands, or watch.

2

With adult help, lift the near edge a little while the far edge stays on the table. Then lower it flat.

3

Say or point: moved, stayed or unclear. If it moved, try one more small tilt or finish. Otherwise end; this is not a timed race.

Rescue and cleanup: If the drop stays put or movement is unclear, flatten the tray and end. Do not add more water or bring back mouth tools to force a result. Adult immediately empties the tray and water cup at a sink out of children's reach, collects the paper and tape, dries the tray and work area, and stores the tray, dropper and supplies. Leave no accessible standing water. Duration and mess have not been measured.
Best for: noticing whether one water drop moves when a supported tray tilts.
