Paper Roll Play

Research by Riley Hart · AI research contributor

One empty roll, one gentle push. Explore how it moves, with an adult beside you.

Research-backed, not family-tested by Kid Activity Lab. Duration and mess have not been measured.

Need1 empty lightweight paper-towel roll and clear, level floor space.

Adult check first

Choose a clean, dry, intact roll without staples, sharp edges, loose covering or attached parts. No hard mailing tubes, blade packaging, small toys, fillers, cords or ramp. If you cannot find a suitable roll, skip.

Clear space away from steps and traffic. Stay beside every reachable child; skip if you cannot watch them all. Try only if the child can leave the roll out of their mouth and respond to your stop cue, with help.

Stop for mouthing, tearing, damage, throwing, unsafe reach, lost adult attention or when the child wants to stop. This is an adult judgment, not a safety assurance.

1

Adult: lay the empty roll on its curved side, opening sideways. Model a gentle push along the floor.

2

Kid mission: push the empty roll and watch it move. Watching an adult push is also an option.

3

Wait until it stops. Retrieve it together and put it back on its side.

4

Push again only if wanted, or finish. No distance, speed or target to reach.

If it does not roll

Adult checks it is on its side and the space is clear, then models a gentle push. The child can watch or point instead. If it is unsuitable or unwanted, stop; do not add toys, a slope or force.

Finish: adult collects the roll and spares, removes damaged material and stores everything away.

Sources and testing status

Northwestern EIRG describes empty-tube rolling. The floor start, script and controls here are Kid Activity Lab's editorial adaptation, not clinical guidance. General material and supervision checks draw on AAP toy guidance and the CPSC art-material guide; they do not certify a reused roll.

Sources checked October 27, 2026. Physical suitability, family understanding, enjoyment, learning and safety outcomes are unknown.
