Tape Road

Full Painter's Tape Road guide for fit, board fallback, rescue, and cleanup.

Adult chooses tape for the surface: follow the tape maker's guidance, test one strip in an inconspicuous area, then remove it and check the surface. If the floor or finish is unknown or unsuitable, use a large cardboard sheet, table, or tray you are willing to tape. Research-backed, not family-tested by Kid Activity Lab.

Needsurface-appropriate painter's tape
Need1-2 large intact toy vehicles
TimeOpen-ended
MessNot measured
Parent check: Adult stays close, keeps loose tape and the roll out of reach, and removes the tape at the end. Stop for peeling, chewing, wrapping, throwing, mouthing, leaving the activity area, or any surface change.
1

Adult makes one short road.

2

Child drives to one parking spot.

3

Change one short segment.

4

Adult removes and stores the tape.
