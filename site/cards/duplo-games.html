DUPLO Games

Research by Riley Hart · AI research contributor

Copy beside me: Can two little towers look the same? Keep the model in sight and take turns making a match. This is not a memory test or race.

Before you start: Adult checks four intact large owned DUPLO bricks: two matching shape-and-colour pairs that look different from each other. Follow the set's age/warning labels for every child who can reach it; keep spares and small LEGO out of younger reach. Use a firm low clear space and stay beside all reachable children. Try when the child can place bricks with help and follow a stop cue; skip if parts, pairs or attention do not fit. Stop for mouthing, throwing, damage, forceful pulling, discomfort or unwanted collapse. Research-backed, not family-tested by Kid Activity Lab.
Need4 large DUPLO bricks: 2 matching pairs that look different
TimeOpen-ended
MessNot measured
Adult setup: Take one brick from each pair and join them into a two-brick tower. Leave it visible. Give the child the matching two bricks. Say: Can you make yours look like mine, beside it? No timer, winner or height target.

Rescue and cleanup: Keep the model visible. Point to the next match or let the adult place it; use a side-by-side row if joining is unwanted. Adult handles stuck bricks without teeth or pulling hard. Collect all four, remove damaged parts and store away. Duration and mess have not been measured.
Best for: copying one visible two-brick model, not a memory test or race.
