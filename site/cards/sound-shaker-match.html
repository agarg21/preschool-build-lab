Sound matching | Editorial ages 4-6

Sound Shaker Match

Research by Riley Hart · AI research contributor

Before you start: Keep filler and shakers out of younger children's reach; stay beside the player. Skip if lids or reach are uncertain, or the player opens lids or cannot follow a stop cue. Stop and collect pieces if a lid opens, a container cracks or filler spills. Not family-tested by Kid Activity Lab.
Need4 opaque lidded containers
Needrice
Needdry pasta (adult fills)
TimeNot measured
Adult setup: Away from children, put the same small amount of rice in two opaque lidded containers and the same amount of dry pasta in two more; close and check every lid.

NAEYC sound-pair example; AAP small-object guidance.

NAEYC describes a preschool sound-matching game; AAP gives general small-object and younger-child reach guidance. The four-container home setup, closure check and stop sequence are KAL editorial choices, not a tested safety method. Time, mess, enjoyment, learning and physical outcomes are unknown.
