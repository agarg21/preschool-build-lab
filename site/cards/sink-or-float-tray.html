Sink or Float Tray

Before you start: Adult checks one intact large water-play toy's age label and condition, and keeps water and toy out of a younger child's reach. No suitable toy? Skip. Also skip if you cannot watch constantly or prevent access. Stop and empty the tray if attention shifts or anyone reaches for water. Not family-tested by Kid Activity Lab.
Needshallow tray, a little water, one intact large bath toy
TimeNot measured
MessNot measured
Adult setup: Put the shallow tray on a stable table with a little water. Adult places and retrieves the toy. If bottom contact is unclear, do not add water to force a result.
1

Guess: up or down?

2

Adult puts toy in; watch.

3

Off bottom? Floats. Drops through water to bottom? Sinks.

4

Unsure? Say 'unclear'; adult empties tray.

Parent check: Adult empties the tray immediately and puts the toy away. Do not leave standing water behind.
Best for: predicting and observing one object's movement in water.
More ways to use this idea: Science experiments for 4 year olds
Sources and testing status

NAEYC sink-or-float example; AAP water supervision; AAP empty-container guidance; CPSC toy age and small-part guidance.
