Open-ended · age 3-6

Sock Ball Roll

Research by Riley Hart · AI research contributor

Before you start: Adult: clear a short floor lane away from stairs, doors, cords, breakables and walkways. Stay beside every child who can reach it; skip if you cannot watch them all. Try when the child can release along the floor and pause on request. Stop for throwing, running, mouthing, a loose sock, discomfort, or anyone entering the lane. Research-backed, not family-tested by Kid Activity Lab.
Needtwo clean soft socks
Needone low empty basket
TimeOpen-ended
MessNot measured
Adult setup: Use socks only, without ties or hard fillers. Push one sock into the other's toe and fold the cuff over it into a soft ball with no trailing fabric. Put the empty basket on the floor nearby. It is a target to roll toward, not a rim to roll over. If the ball comes apart, stop and remake it.
1

Put the sock ball on the floor.

2

Roll gently toward the basket; touching it is optional.

3

Pause, then walk to collect the ball and reset.

Parent check: If aiming stalls, bring the basket closer or gently roll to an adult partner instead. No basket? Use that partner-roll option in the same clear lane. Take turns one at a time; do not lengthen the lane beyond adult reach. End by putting away the socks and basket and clearing the floor.
Best for: aiming one gentle floor roll toward a target.
