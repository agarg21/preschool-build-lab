Same car | Adult steadies

Ramp Texture Test

Research by Riley Hart · AI research contributor

Basic ramp setup and troubleshooting. The guide compares landing surfaces, not a cloth covering. Deeper surface-observation routine.

Need: 1 flat stiff cardboard piece, 2 broad sturdy closed books, 1 owned toy car, 1 optional thin flat dish towel, owned masking tape.
Open-ended; duration and mess not measured.

Before you start

Materials: Adult checks intact cardboard, books, towel and freely turning car wheels; follow the owned toy and tape labels. Use a car appropriate for every child who can reach it, without loose parts. Adult handles tape, spare materials and cover changes.

Adult and space: Floor only, with a clear landing and walking path. Adult stays beside every reachable child and steadies the ramp throughout. Retrieve the car only after it stops; no climbing, standing on books or ramp, throwing or chasing.

Fit or skip: Try when the child can let go without pushing and follow a stop cue, or watch the adult release. Pointing instead of touching the towel is optional. Skip if space, mouthing, material labels, stability or attention do not fit.

Evidence: Research-backed, not family-tested by Kid Activity Lab.

Stop: End if the ramp or books slip, cardboard bends, the towel bunches or comes loose, a part loosens, anyone mouths materials, climbs, throws, becomes uncomfortable or attention shifts. No forcing a launch or raising the ramp to get a result.

Adult setup: On the floor, stack the two books flat and lean one cardboard end on them, the other on the floor. Check support and hold it steady. Adult first releases the car without pushing from one chosen spot. If it will not freely roll or the setup will not stay stable, skip the comparison; do not keep adding height. Keep tape and spares out of reach. Choose one start spot to use for both rolls.
1

Adult steadies the plain ramp. Let go of the car at the chosen spot without pushing, or watch the adult. Wait until it stops before retrieving.

2

Adult covers the wheel path with the thin towel and secures it flat with masking tape, following owned labels. Keep the same books and height. Adult checks for loose edges, folds or catches; if it will not stay flat, skip this roll and finish.

3

With the ramp still steadied, release the same car from the same spot without pushing. Notice: nearer, farther, about the same, stuck or unclear. One comparison is enough; you can finish.

Rescue and cleanup: If stuck or unclear, finish rather than push harder or raise the ramp. Adult removes the cover; return to one plain roll only if stable and wanted, or end. Adult promptly puts away the car, tape, towel, books and cardboard and clears the floor. Duration and mess are not measured; a single result does not prove friction alone caused it.
Best for: noticing where the same car stops on a plain ramp and one optional covered ramp.
