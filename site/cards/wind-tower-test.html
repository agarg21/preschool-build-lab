Build low | Adult fans

Wind Tower Test

Research by Riley Hart · AI research contributor

Need: 6 matching intact lightweight nonmagnetic blocks with no small or detachable parts, 1 adult-prepared folded paper fan.
Open-ended; duration and mess not measured.

Before you start

Materials: Follow the owned toy labels and check blocks and paper for damage or loose pieces. Adult folds the paper fan and controls it and spares. No electric fan, hairdryer, heavy book or mouth blowing.

Adult and space: Use a level floor away from walking paths. Adult stays beside every reachable child. Before fanning, move everyone's hands and bodies clear of possible falling pieces; leave room to wave without touching blocks or people. Retrieve only after the fan stops and pieces settle.

Fit or skip: Try when the child can place blocks gently and follow a stop cue, or point and watch the adult. Skip if labels, mouthing, space, attention or keeping the fan clear do not fit. Stop if falling or noise is unwelcome.

Evidence: Research-backed, not family-tested by Kid Activity Lab. Counts, layout and waves are editorial choices, not calibrated wind or tested protection.

Stop: End for loose parts, mouthing, throwing, climbing, fan contact risk, discomfort or shifted attention. Stop fanning sooner if pieces move or fall, or anyone wants to stop. Never fan harder, move closer or build taller to force collapse.

Adult setup: Adult checks a flat three-block base on the floor. Add up to three blocks on top only if steady and wanted; use no more than two layers and keep below the child's chest. If the blocks will not sit flat, use a single row or skip fanning. Choose one fan position with room for a small clear wave and keep it for any repeat. Keep spares out of reach.
1

Place three blocks side by side. With adult help, add up to three on top only if steady, or watch. Keep the build below the child's chest.

2

Move hands and bodies clear. Adult alone makes up to three small slow paper-fan waves from the chosen spot, without touching blocks or people. Stop sooner if pieces move or fall, or anyone wants to stop.

3

Notice or point: standing, moved, fell or unclear. Wait for the fan to stop and pieces to settle before retrieving. One fan test is enough; you can finish.

Rescue and cleanup: If nothing moves or the result is unclear, finish without stronger or closer fanning. If wanted, adult helps make a lower or wider build from the same blocks and repeats from the same spot with the same gentle waves; force is not calibrated. Otherwise keep a flat row or end. Adult promptly puts the fan and blocks away and clears the floor. Duration and mess are not measured.
Best for: noticing whether a low block build stands, moves or falls during adult fanning.
