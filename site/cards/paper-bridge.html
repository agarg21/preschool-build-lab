Paper Bridge

Full Paper Bridge guide for the optional fold, rescue, and cleanup. Research-backed; not family-tested by Kid Activity Lab.

Fit: Choose one intact large lightweight block or soft toy suitable for every child who can reach it. Try when your child can place it gently and follow a stop cue. Adult sets the low books. If a younger child mouths materials and you cannot keep spare materials out of reach while attending to both, skip.

Parent check: Stay beside the setup. Stop if books slide, the object is thrown, paper goes in a mouth or tears, or the setup becomes climbing play. Remove loose paper pieces before continuing.
