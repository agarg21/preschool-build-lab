Open-ended · age 4-6

Paper Bridge

Needpaper
Needtwo low closed books
Needlarge lightweight object
TimeOpen-ended
1

Set two books low.

2

Lay one paper sheet across.

3

Place one large object gently.

4

Fold the paper and compare.

Parent check: Stay close. Stop if books slide, paper tears, the object is thrown, or the setup becomes climbing play.
Best for: building, comparing, changing one thing.
