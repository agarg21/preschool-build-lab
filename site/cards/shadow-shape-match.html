Adult-controlled light | One block

Shadow Builder

Research by Riley Hart · AI research contributor

Need: 1 owned flashlight, 1 checked large opaque block, clear wall.
Open-ended; duration and mess not measured.

Before you start

Materials: Adult checks the flashlight's instructions, intact casing and closed, secured battery compartment. Use it only as its instructions permit; no lasers, candles or exposed bulbs. Check the block's age/warning labels for every reachable child; use a large intact opaque (not see-through) block without loose small parts, magnets or battery parts.

Adult and space: Keep light on wall, never faces. Stay beside every reachable child and keep the light and spare pieces under adult control. Keep a clear walking path visible; dim only enough to see the shadow.

Fit or skip: Try when the child can move the block with help and follow a stop cue; watching or pointing is an option. Skip if materials, steady wall beam, space or adult attention do not fit, or dimness is unwanted.

Evidence: Research-backed, not family-tested by Kid Activity Lab.

Stop: Switch off if the beam leaves the wall, a child reaches for the light or battery parts, a piece is mouthed or thrown, attention shifts, or anyone is uncomfortable.

Adult setup: Set the flashlight on a stable low surface pointing at the clear wall, away from the walking path. Adult checks that it stays still and makes a visible shadow when the block is between light and wall. Keep the light fixed; start the block nearer the wall, leaving room to move it toward the light without touching the flashlight. If this cannot be arranged, skip.
1

Hold the checked block between the steady light and wall, nearer the wall.

2

Move the block a little nearer the light, not the light itself. Look for a bigger shadow.

3

Move the block back. Say or point: bigger, smaller or unclear? Repeat or finish.

Rescue and cleanup: If the shadow is unclear or the flashlight becomes the toy, switch it off and park it. Adult checks the opaque block is between light and wall. Resume only after an adult restores a steady wall-pointed beam and a visible shadow; the child moves the block or watches the adult move it. Otherwise end. Brighten the room if wanted. Adult switches off and puts away the light, block and spares, clearing the walking path. Duration and mess have not been measured.
Best for: noticing how moving one object changes its shadow, not a matching game or test.
