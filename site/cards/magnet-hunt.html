Do not start this version.

This card is paused. Do not use a known or suspected high-powered or rare-earth magnet, or one with unknown type and power.

Choose a different start

Why this card is paused

Use the owned product's documentation to establish type and power; a large size, intact appearance or age label alone does not settle this. Also pause for small, loose, cracked or missing magnets, younger-child reach or divided adult attention. Even if you can identify the magnet, this card does not currently offer a runnable setup; we have not checked an owned magnet or household test surface.

AAP magnetic-toy guidance warns about high-powered and loose magnets. CPSC magnet guidance describes ingestion hazards. These sources do not establish that an unspecified magnet in a home is suitable for this activity. The pause and alternate route are Kid Activity Lab editorial decisions; this version has not been family- or physically tested.
