Ball Maze Box

Needshallow box lid
Needchunky blocks
Needlarge lightweight ball
TimeOpen-ended
1

Put the lid flat.

2

Set three blocks as wide walls.

3

Adult adds one large ball.

4

Tilt gently, then reset.

Parent check: Adult controls the ball and stays close. Stop after throwing, mouthing, hard shaking, a spill, or damaged material.
Best for: building a path, rolling, changing one wall.
