One AB pair | Adult sets choices

Pattern Path

Research by Riley Hart · AI research contributor

Preschool pattern start and diagram.

Need: 6 intact lightweight nonmagnetic blocks: 3 of kind A and 3 of kind B, visibly different in shape, type or color; no small or detachable parts.
Open-ended; duration and mess not measured.

Before you start

Materials: Adult follows owned toy labels and checks condition and suitability for every reachable child. Size alone is not assurance. Use two kinds the child can distinguish without naming colors.

Adult and space: Use a stable tabletop away from edges. This is a row of blocks, not a walking path. Adult stays beside every reachable child and controls spare pieces.

Fit or skip: Try when the child can match two kinds and gently place a piece, or point for the adult. Otherwise match two alike, watch or end. Skip if labels, mouthing, visibility or attention do not fit.

Evidence: Research-backed, not family-tested by Kid Activity Lab. Counts, script, retry and controls are editorial choices.

Stop: End for mouthing, throwing, damaged or loose parts, discomfort or shifted attention, or whenever the child wants to stop. Adult gathers all pieces and puts them away.

Adult setup: Call the two kinds A and B. Lay four blocks A-B-A-B, using two of each kind. Name or point to the AB pair once. Offer the remaining A and B together and ask what comes next; do not supply the answer immediately.
1

Look along A-B-A-B. Choose the next piece from the adult's A and B choices, or point for the adult to place it.

2

If it continues the pattern, say or point to the AB part that repeats. No color names are needed.

3

One extension is enough. You can watch, stop or help the adult collect the blocks; no need to keep repeating.

Rescue and cleanup: If B is chosen, point to the first AB pair and compare the second; offer one supported retry. Then end or match two alike with the adult. Matching is not a completed repeating pattern. No response is a reason to watch or end, not drill or assess. Adult collects and stores all blocks. Duration and mess are not measured.
Best for: noticing and continuing one repeating AB pair.
