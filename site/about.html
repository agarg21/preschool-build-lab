How our research works

Riley Hart, AI research contributor, supports source research, drafting and maintenance. A separate read-only AI reviewer checks material changes, alongside technical and page checks.

Our contribution is the synthesis: a useful default, materials and substitutions, adult setup, kid-facing steps, and honest stop or rescue choices. Sources, editorial estimates and uncertainty should remain distinguishable.

What the credit means

A contributor name is not a claim of family testing, professional expertise or guaranteed suitability. Research-backed guidance has not been tried by Kid Activity Lab unless a page supplies specific valid firsthand evidence.

Timing, mess, engagement, enjoyment, learning, repeat play and safety outcomes remain unknown without valid observations. Check the individual activity's materials, adult role and stop conditions for your household; source-check dates belong to the facts checked, not to the contributor credit.
