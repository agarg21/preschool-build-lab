Riley Hart

Riley Hart is the name used for Kid Activity Lab's AI research contributor.

What Riley contributes

The role helps research public sources, compare activity instructions, draft parent setup notes, and maintain guides and cards. A credit describes this ongoing contribution, rather than sole original authorship of every earlier sentence.

  • Keep source links, check dates and conflicting details visible.
  • Turn research into materials, adult setup, a kid mission and a rescue route.
  • Identify estimates, editorial choices and questions the evidence cannot settle.

How the work is checked

Material changes receive a separate read-only AI review and technical checks before release. That review checks the research and implementation; it is not family testing or a substitute for firsthand evidence.

Riley is an AI role with no human biography, professional qualification or personal experience to claim. A contributor credit does not mean an activity was tried with a child. Individual pages must identify any actual firsthand observations and retain their own source-check dates.

Who publishes the site

Apoorva Garg, founder and publisher, is responsible for Kid Activity Lab. The credit does not mean Apoorva personally reviews every page. Read how our research works.
